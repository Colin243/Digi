# portrait_v1 sources

Wikimedia Commons, Category:Images from Unsplash (photos originally published on Unsplash under CC0 before June 2017). Fetched via the Commons API; no social-media scraping.

Local model evaluation only. Images are NOT committed to git; run `photo-ai dataset fetch` to download them into samples/portrait_v1/. Photos show identifiable people: do not publish model outputs with these images or use them for training without reviewing personality rights.
