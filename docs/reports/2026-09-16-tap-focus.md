# Tap-to-focus and hold

The native viewfinder now accepts taps through its UIKit preview surface. AVCaptureVideoPreviewLayer converts visible layer coordinates to sensor coordinates, taking video gravity and connection rotation into account. Taps outside the displayed video rectangle are ignored. Filtered preview pixels and the grid allow hit testing through to this surface.

The controller freezes the selected point into a request on the serial capture queue. It validates capability and normalized coordinates, locks device configuration, sets focusPointOfInterest and requests autoFocus. Apple defines autoFocus as adjusting once then locking; this avoids guessing lens position or setting a timer to approximate convergence. An amber AF square represents the requested point, not proof of optical sharpness. It does not track a person.

The marker is drawn above the filtered image. Its display position is calculated back from camera coordinates after preview layout/rotation. Auto focus in Shooting options restores centered continuousAutoFocus and removes the marker. Leaving the app resets focus. Exposure remains automatic; no AE lock or face reshaping is introduced. Taps are ignored while capturing/saving, with a pending save, or without a supported active camera.

Signed device build and whitespace validation passed. Simulator checks exercise default focus/reset availability and existing controls/rotation, but cannot establish physical lens movement, acquisition quality or tap alignment on a camera feed. On the iPhone, tap a close object, reframe toward a distant object and confirm focus remains held; tap the distant object to refocus; use Auto focus to resume continuous adjustment. Repeat portrait and both landscapes, with G12 Look on and off.
