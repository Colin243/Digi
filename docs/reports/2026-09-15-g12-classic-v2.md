# G12-inspired Classic v2

The previous unpaired fit made only subtle visible changes. Classic v2 retains that fitted base and adds an explicitly designed contrast and selective-color layer. This is an aesthetic iteration, not evidence of improved physical G12 accuracy.

## Processing

The preserved base is `research/regression/profiles/g12-fitted-v1.json`: eight iPhone source photos and 33 G12 originals in 17 scene groups. `research/regression/design_look.py` adds endpoint-preserving contrast coefficients [0.025, 0.85], six hue-dependent saturation multipliers [1.10, 1.02, 1.13, 1.06, 1.08, 1.01], and small luma-neutral hue offsets attenuated near neutrals and highlights. Chroma is compressed to remain in gamut. No synthetic grain, fake flash, or scene classifier is added.

A 65³ linear-sRGB LUT is packaged in the app and shared by preview and saved JPEGs on iPhone 17 Pro. Other hardware remains on the original-output path until profiled. The G12 Look toggle permits comparison with original output. This LUT responds to pixel colors and brightness; it does not infer actual illumination or undo the iPhone imaging pipeline.

## Visual checks

Five comparison sheets in `output/g12-v3/` compare original, fitted v1, and Classic v2 across food, city backlight, outdoor landscape, window-lit portrait, and bedroom scenes. The food, landscape and portrait sheets were inspected. Classic is visibly more contrasty and colorful; shadow detail can become less prominent. These are held-out aesthetic inspections, not paired-camera accuracy measurements.

## Icon

`research/design/digi_icon.py` produces the original opaque 1024px icon in the native asset catalog: charcoal camera body, silver lens ring, teal glass, amber indicator and D monogram. No Canon branding or reference photographs are bundled. The signed build includes the AppIcon asset.
